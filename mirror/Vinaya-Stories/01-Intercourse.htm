01 The Rule about Intercourse


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Rule about Intercourse from Stories from the Vinayapiṭaka together with further information.

Sudinna, a merchant’s son, starves himself until his parents permit his ordination. During a famine, he visits his family in Vesālī to secure alms for monks. Urged by his mother to produce an heir, Sudinna engages in sexual intercourse with his former wife, conceiving Bījaka. Overcome with remorse, he confesses. The Buddha sharply rebukes his conduct, leading to the formulation of the first parājika training rule against sexual intercourse.
