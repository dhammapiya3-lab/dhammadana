25 Wrong Views


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Wrong Views from Stories from the Vinayapiṭaka together with further information.

The monk Ariṭṭha, formerly a vulture-killer, holds a wrong view: that sense pleasures, declared as obstructions by the Buddha, are not actually sufficient to obstruct one indulging in them. Monks try to dissuade him, but he clings to his view. The Buddha rebukes him and instructs the Community to suspend him for not giving up the evil view.
