04 The Rule about False Claims


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Rule about False Claims from Stories from the Vinayapiṭaka together with further information.

During a famine, monks along the Vaggumudā River agree to falsely praise each other’s spiritual attainments to local laypeople to secure abundant almsfood. While other monks suffer from starvation, these monks thrive on stolen offerings. Upon uncovering their deception, the Buddha severely rebukes them, identifies false claimants of spiritual attainments as the supreme thieves in the world, and establishes the fourth parājika rule prohibiting false claims of super-human achievements.
