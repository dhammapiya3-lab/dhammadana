39 Events Surrounding the Second Council


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Events Surrounding the Second Council from Stories from the Vinayapiṭaka together with further information.

A century after the Buddha’s Nibbāna, Vesālian monks adopt ten lax practices, including accepting gold and silver. The elder Yasa publicly rejects this, is reconciled, and travels to gather support. Seven hundred arahants, led by Revata and Sabbakāmī, convene at Vesālī. They examine each of the ten practices, find them against the Teaching, and reject them. This recitation is called the Recitation of the Seven Hundred.
