18 The Rift at Kosambī


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Rift at Kosambī from Stories from the Vinayapiṭaka together with further information.

A dispute arises over whether a monk’s action was an offence. Monks suspend him, but he gains supporters, causing a schism. The Buddha warns against suspension that divides the community and urges reconciliation. The monks ignore his counsel. The Buddha departs alone, reciting verses on how enmity is only appeased by non-enmity and the value of solitary practice.
