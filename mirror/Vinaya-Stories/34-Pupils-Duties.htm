34 Pupils’ Duties to Preceptors


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Pupils’ Duties to Preceptors from Stories from the Vinayapiṭaka together with further information.

Co-resident pupils fail to behave properly toward their preceptors. The Buddha rebukes them and lays down detailed duties: providing toothbrush, water, seat, and gruel in the morning; assisting with robes and bowl; accompanying them to the village; preparing seats and water on return; washing and storing bowls and robes; attending them in sickness; and caring for them throughout life.
