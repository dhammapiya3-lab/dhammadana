10 Perseverance


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Perseverance from The Life of Milarepa together with further information.

Moved by remorse and renunciation, Milarepa endures extreme austerity in mountain retreats, surviving on nettles. Despite poverty, abuse, and family conflict, he perseveres in meditation. Through the lama’s guidance, his practice deepens, yielding profound realization. He teaches his sister Peta and aunt, emphasizing conviction in cause and effect, renunciation of worldly concerns, and unwavering perseverance as essential for attaining buddhahood in one lifetime.
