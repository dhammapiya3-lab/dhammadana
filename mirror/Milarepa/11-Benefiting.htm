11 Benefiting the Teachings


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Benefiting the Teachings from The Life of Milarepa together with further information.

Rechungpa asks Milarepa about the episodes of his life that inspire laughter. The Jetsün explains these are the times he established fortunate disciples—human and non-human—on the path of ripening and liberation, thereby benefiting the Buddha’s teachings. He names his many retreat places and recounts meeting his heart-disciples, including Rechungpa, Gampopa, and Tseringma.
