high-definition creative commons posters depicting Activities from The Life of Milarepa together with further information.

Geshe Tsakpuwa, jealous of Milarepa, sends a woman with poisoned curds. Knowing his time has come, Milarepa accepts the poison. He teaches his disciples, transfers his illness to demonstrate his power, and forgives the Geshe. Before passing, he gives final instructions, distributing his few possessions. At cremation, dakinis carry his relics to a pure land.
