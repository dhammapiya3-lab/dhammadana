13 Disciples and Colophon


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Disciples and Colophon from The Life of Milarepa together with further information.

This closing section lists Milarepa’s disciples—eight heart-sons including Gampopa and Rechungpa, thirteen close-sons, four female disciples, and countless human and non-human followers. After the funeral rites, the disciples departed to mountain retreats. The text describes Milarepa’s life as twelve marvelous deeds—three worldly and nine supreme—and concludes with verses praising the biography’s power to inspire faith, renunciation, and liberation.
