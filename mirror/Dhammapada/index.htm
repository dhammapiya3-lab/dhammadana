Watercolour Style

Illustrated Stories from the Dhammapada Commentary, which relates many events from the life of the Buddha and his disciples.
Source of the Stories

These storybooks illustrate the Dhammapada Commentary, which is a large collection of stories that provide the historical and situational context for each verse in the Dhammapada. Tradition attributes the compilation of the commentary to the 5th-century scholar Buddhaghosa. It explains the specific person, place, and event that prompted the Buddha to speak each verse. It contains over 300 stories detailing the lives, triumphs, and struggles of monks, laypeople, and historical figures during the Buddha’s time. It is a repository of knowledge about the people who were around the Buddha while he was alive; and it also contains a plethora of information about former Buddhas and the previous lives of the Buddha and his disciples.
