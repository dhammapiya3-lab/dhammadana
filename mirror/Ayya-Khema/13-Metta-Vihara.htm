13 Mettā Vihāra


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Mettā Vihāra from The Life of Ayyā Khemā together with further information.

In 1989, Ayyā Khemā established a spiritual center in Germany with help from dedicated students. Prompted by a cancer diagnosis and a sense of spiritual urgency, Ayyā offered meditation courses, published books, and promoted interreligious dialogue. The movement expanded rapidly, leading to an urban center in Munich and the founding of Mettā Vihāra, Germany’s first Buddhist forest monastery, providing a peaceful sanctuary for deep meditation, reflection, and growth.
