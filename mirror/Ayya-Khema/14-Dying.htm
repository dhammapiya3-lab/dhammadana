14 Preparing for Death


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Preparing for Death from The Life of Ayyā Khemā together with further information.

Ayyā Khemā reflects on death through Buddhist teachings, drawing from Siddhattha Gotama’s realization of human impermanence upon encountering aging, sickness, and mortality. True peace comes from letting go of the ego, desires, and the fear of death. Caring for the dying requires offering physical comfort, relieving pain, reminding them of good deeds, and allowing them to pass peacefully without holding on, ultimately achieving Nibbāna, which is complete peace and total liberation.
