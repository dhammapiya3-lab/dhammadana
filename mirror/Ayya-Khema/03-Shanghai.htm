03 War Years in Shanghai


high-definition creative commons posters depicting War Years in Shanghai from The Life of Ayyā Khemā together with further information.

Between 1938 and 1941, twenty thousand European Jews fled Nazi persecution to Shanghai, an open city requiring no entrance visa. The author describes adapting to life in Shanghai, attending business school, and later enduring confinement in the Hongkew ghetto during the Japanese occupation. Despite harsh living conditions, bombing raids, and her father’s tragic death, she persevered, worked for the American army, married, and eventually emigrated to San Francisco in 1949.
