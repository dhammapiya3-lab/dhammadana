04 New Ways, New Worlds


high-definition creative commons posters depicting New Ways, New Worlds from The Life of Ayyā Khemā together with further information.

In 1949, Jewish refugees arrived in San Francisco from Shanghai, relocating to Los Angeles to build new lives. Despite achieving material stability and raising a family, the author felt a deep spiritual void. Reconnecting with a childhood acquaintance inspired a search for meaning, prompting her to separate from her husband. Moving to a Mexican health retreat, she embraced spiritual study, married her second husband Gerd, and eventually set off toward broad new journeys.
