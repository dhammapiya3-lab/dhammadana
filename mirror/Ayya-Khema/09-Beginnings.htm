09 The Beginning of My Path


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Beginning of My Path from The Life of Ayyā Khemā together with further information.

Ilse recounts traveling through Asia to Australia with her family. In India, she learned simple meditation from the Mother at Sri Aurobindo ashram, initiating a lifelong spiritual practice. After journeying across Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia, the family settled in Queensland to establish an organic farm. A dramatic moment when her son fell from a horse helped her release inner fear, allowing her to cultivate spiritual freedom and unconditional love.
