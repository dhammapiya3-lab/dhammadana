These storybooks are based on an English translation of the Thūpavaṁsa, The Legend of the Thūpas, which begins with the legendary lineages of past Buddhas, detailing their spiritual aspirations and the monuments erected in their honor across India. It specifically traces the life of Gotama Buddha, his enlightenment, and the subsequent distribution of his relics among various nations for veneration. The narrative then shifts to Ceylon, documenting the arrival of sacred relics and the pious efforts of kings like Duṭṭhagāmaṇi to construct magnificent stupas. By combining mythology with genealogical records, the source serves as a comprehensive guide to the architectural and devotional history of the Buddhist faith.
