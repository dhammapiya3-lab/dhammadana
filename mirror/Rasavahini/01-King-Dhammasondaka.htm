01 King Dhammasoṇḍaka


high-definition creative commons posters depicting King Dhammasoṇḍaka Story from the Rasavāhiṇī (Heart Streams) together with further information.

King Dhammasoṇḍaka of Benares felt royal glory was hollow without the Buddha’s Dhamma. Seeking truth, he entered the forest where Sakra tested him disguised as a terrifying demon. The demon offered a sacred verse in exchange for the King’s life. Driven by a quest for omniscience, the King joyfully leaped from a cliff. Sakra caught him, revealed his identity, and taught him the profound truth concerning the impermanence of things.
