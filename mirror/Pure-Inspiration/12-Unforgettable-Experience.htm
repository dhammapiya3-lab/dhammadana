An Unforgettable Experience


high-definition creative commons posters depicting An Unforgettable Experience by Ven. Pannipitiye Upasama and Nimal Sonnadara together with further information.

Two devoted lay attendants recall looking after Ven. Ñāṇavimala during his final years on Parappaduwa Island. They witness the extraordinary power of his trained mind, highlighting an instance where he comfortably handled a major health crisis without food, purely by observing his own consciousness. Reflecting on his German youth, wartime experiences, and absolute renunciation, they detail his ultimate triumph of successfully abandoning all life-support medications while remaining completely healthy.
