Finding the Path


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Finding the Path by Ishanka together with further information.

An anonymous Sri Lankan laywoman recounts how Ven. Ñāṇavimala’s compassionate guidance rescued her from a severe spiral of depression following a broken relationship. Over a span of twenty years, the monk provided tailored spiritual counsel, teaching her to systematically cultivate loving-kindness (mettā) and investigate the suttas. The narrative highlights his ability to trigger remarkable personal transformations, which even helped turn an alcoholic relative into a model, sober citizen.
