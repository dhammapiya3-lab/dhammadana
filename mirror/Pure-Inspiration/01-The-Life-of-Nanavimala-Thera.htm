The Life of Ñāṇavimala Thera


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Life of Ñāṇavimala Thera by Ven. Bhikkhu Bodhi & Ven. Bhikkhu Ñāṇatusita together with further information.

This outlines the biography of Friedrich Möller, a German-born seeker who arrived in Sri Lanka, became Ven. Ñāṇavimala, and emerged as the world’s most senior Western monk. After a decade of strict study at the Island Hermitage, he spent twenty-five years on a continuous walking tour (cārikā) across Sri Lanka. Evading possessions and social attachments, his barefoot, nomadic lifestyle exemplified the ultimate Buddhist goal of radical renunciation and Nibbāna.
