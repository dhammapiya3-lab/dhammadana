08 The Last Years


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Last Years together with further information.

This covers the death of his beloved queen Asandhimittā (yr 29), the rise of his jealous consort Tiṣyarakṣitā (yr 32), and her destruction of the sacred Bodhi-tree (yr 34). Northern sources recount court intrigue involving his son Kuṇāla and his own loss of power, culminating in his death (yr 37). His demise led to a disputed succession and the rapid fragmentation of his once-mighty empire.
