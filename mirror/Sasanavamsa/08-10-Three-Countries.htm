08-10 Mahiṁsaka, Mahāraṭṭha and Cīna


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Mahiṁsaka, Mahāraṭṭha and Cīna from The History of the Buddha’s Religion together with further information.

This section chronicles the expansion of the sāsana across three distinct regions: Mahiṁsaka-maṇḍala, Mahāraṭṭha and “Cīna”, the Himalayan regions. Led by the elders Mahādeva, Yonaka Dhammarakkhita, and Mahādhammarakkhita respectively, each mission utilized core discourses like the Devadūta Sutta and Aggikkhandhopama Sutta to prompt profound spiritual awakenings, widespread local conversions, and the establishment of vibrant regional monastic lineages.
