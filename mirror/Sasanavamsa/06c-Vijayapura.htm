06c Vijayapura


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Vijayapura from The History of the Buddha’s Religion together with further information.

This text chronicles the history of Buddhism in Vijayapura (Pinya) and surrounding northern Burmese regions following the shift of political power from Pagan. It highlights how local rulers sustained the sāsana through royal endowments, monastery construction, and active support for scholarly monks, ensuring the continuity of monastic lineages and scriptural learning during political transitions.
