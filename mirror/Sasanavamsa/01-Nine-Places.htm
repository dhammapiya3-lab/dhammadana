high-definition creative commons posters depicting Nine Places from The History of the Buddha’s Religion from The History of the Buddha’s Religion together with further information.

Following the Third Buddhist Council led by Moggaliputta Tissa Mahāthera, nine missions were dispatched to establish the Buddha’s dispensation (sāsana) in different regions. This text details those nine specific destinations, the elder monks who led each mission, and the thousands of locals who converted, laying the groundwork for Buddhism’s global expansion beyond its foundational borders in India.
