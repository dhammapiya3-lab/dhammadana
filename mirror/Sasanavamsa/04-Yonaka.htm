04 Yonaka


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Yonaka from The History of the Buddha’s Religion together with further information.

This section outlines the Buddhist mission to Yonakaraṭṭha (idetified here as Harinjuñja in Lannā), led by the elder Mahārakkhita. It describes his discourse on the Kālakārāma Sutta, which inspired widespread spiritual awakening, resulting in thousands of ordinations and establishing a robust, distinct monastic presence in what was later Thailand.
