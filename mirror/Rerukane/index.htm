Source of the Stories

These storybooks chronicle the remarkable life of Ven. Rerukane Candavimala, a Buddhist monk who rose from a humble upbringing and minimal schooling to become a preeminent scholar. After traveling to Burma for his early religious training, he returned to Sri Lanka where he utilized his exceptional memory and intellectual depth to teach and write influential texts. His journey included managing various temple headquarters, revitalizing regional religious centers, and bridging gaps between different monastic traditions. Despite his early struggles with formal education, his dedication to contemplating intricate spiritual concepts led to a prolific writing career and significant leadership roles. Ultimately, his contributions to the Sāsana earned him national respect and honorary academic accolades, marking his transformation into a revered literary figure.
