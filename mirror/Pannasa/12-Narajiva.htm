12 Narajīva


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Narajīva from Fifty Lannā Jātaka Stories together with further information.

In a past life, the Bodhisatta, Narajīva, inspired a pious householder to offer sacred kaṭhina cloth to Buddha Padumuttara, earning a prophecy of his future Buddhahood as Gotama. The immense karmic merits of this generous act, alongside their past friendships, shaped the subsequent rebirths of Narajīva and his companions. This profound Jātaka story beautifully illustrates how deeds of generosity and moral choices bear powerful, enduring fruits across multiple lifetimes.
