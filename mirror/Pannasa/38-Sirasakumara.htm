38 Prince Sirasā


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Prince Sirasā from Fifty Lannā Jātaka Stories together with further information.

Devadatta’s enmity toward the Buddha prompts this tale. King Paṭhama’s son, Sirasākumāra, is born miraculously and later floated down the Ganges with his mother due to a wicked Brahman’s advice. Saved by a loyal army chief, the boy reveals he is a Buddha-sprout, teaches Dhamma, and returns to Tāvatiṁsa heaven, reborn as a god.
