45 Ungratefulness


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Ungratefulness together with further information.

A crow makes a poor fisherman king by hiding him in a chest and claiming he returned from the dead. The king prospers but forgets his promise to feed the crow, who then carries him back to his fishing spot. The Buddha teaches that ungrateful people are censured and reborn in hell, while the grateful prosper. Devadatta is identified as the ungrateful king.
