18. The Relinquishment of the Life Process


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Relinquishment of the Life Process together with further information.

After Ānanda departs, Māra approaches the Buddha and urges him to enter Parinibbāna. The Buddha agrees, stating that his mission is complete and his disciples are well-trained. He formally relinquishes his will to live, an act that causes a great earthquake and thunder. When Ānanda later realises his mistake and begs the Buddha to stay, the Buddha refuses, explaining that the time for such a request has passed and all things must end.
