36. Ānanda’s Marvellous Qualities


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Ānanda’s Marvellous Qualities together with further information.

Overcome with grief, Ānanda leans against a doorpost and weeps. The Buddha calls for him, offering comfort by reminding him of the law of impermanence. He then praises Ānanda before the assembly, highlighting his four wonderful qualities: his popularity, his skill in teaching, his remarkable memory, and his ability to know the right time for people to meet the Buddha. He encourages Ānanda to strive diligently, predicting he will soon reach Awakening.
