The Lives of the Foremost Nuns

Source of the Stories

These posters chronicle the extraordinary biographies and past-life aspirations of 13 prominent female disciples of the Buddha. Each narrative follows a similar spiritual trajectory, beginning with an ancient vow made before a previous Buddha to achieve a specific area of excellence. The text details the lives of figures such as Mahā Pajāpati Gotamī, the founder of the nuns’ order, and Ven. Khemā, celebrated for her profound wisdom. Other accounts, like those of Kisā Gotamī and Uppalavaṇṇā, emphasize how personal tragedies and karmic shifts eventually led them to spiritual awakening. These records highlight the diverse backgrounds of these women, ranging from royalty to laborers, and their shared dedication to the monastic life. The collection serves to document the foremost titles granted to these nuns for their unique mastery of the Buddhist path.
