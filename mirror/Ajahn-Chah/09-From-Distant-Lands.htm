9. From Distant Lands


high-definition creative commons posters depicting From Distant Lands from Stillness Flowing, The Life and Teachings of Ajahn Chah together with further information.

This section explores Ajahn Chah’s unique relationship with the Western Sangha. It details how foreign seekers arrived at Wat Pah Pong, adapted to the strict monastic discipline, and navigated cultural barriers. Ajahn’s universal, practical approach to the Dhamma helped establish a thriving international community, ultimately leading to the expansion of the forest tradition to Western countries.
