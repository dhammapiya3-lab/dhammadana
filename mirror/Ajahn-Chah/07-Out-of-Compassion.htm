7. Out of Compassion


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Out of Compassion from Stillness Flowing, The Life and Teachings of Ajahn Chah together with further information.

This part of the story focuses on Ajahn Chah’s deep relationship with the lay community. Driven by compassion, he taught lay practitioners how to bring Dhamma practice into daily life. By offering practical advice, conducting teachings, and establishing clear behavioral standards, he guided householders to overcome worldly suffering, abandon harmful habits, cultivate internal virtue, and discover peace within their own minds.
