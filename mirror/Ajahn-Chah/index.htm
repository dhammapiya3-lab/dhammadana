Source of the Stories (note that chapter order is different here)

These storybooks provide a comprehensive biographical and spiritual overview of the renowned Thai Buddhist master Ajahn Chah, tracing his journey from his early ascetic training in the forest tradition to his global influence. The chapters document the founding of Wat Nong Pah Pong, where he established a community rooted in rigorous monastic discipline and the integration of mindfulness into all aspects of life. His teachings reached a diverse audience, including Western seekers, female renunciants, and the lay community, bridging cultural gaps through a universal approach to ending suffering. The collection also details his international travels to England and America, which laid the groundwork for a thriving worldwide lineage. Finally, the narrative explores his later years, illustrating how he faced physical decline with equanimity and how his eventual passing inspired profound devotion and unity among his disciples. Together, these books present a visual and textual record of a life dedicated to spiritual awakening and the preservation of the Dhamma.
