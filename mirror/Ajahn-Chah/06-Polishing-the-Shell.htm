6. Polishing the Shell


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Polishing the Shell from Stillness Flowing, The Life and Teachings of Ajahn Chah together with further information.

Focusing on the practical training of monks, this chapter highlights the austere lifestyle and psychological tools Luang Por Chah used to break down his disciples’ egos and defilements. Through community work, enduring physical discomfort, strict adherence to rules, and constant self-reflection, monastics learned to let go of pride and worldly values to purify their minds like a polished shell.
