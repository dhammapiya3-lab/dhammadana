2. Off the Beaten Track


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Off the Beaten Track from Stillness Flowing, The Life and Teachings of Ajahn Chah together with further information.

This chapter chronicles the founding and early establishment of Wat Nong Pah Pong in March 1954. It describes how Ajahn Chah and his initial disciples settled into the dense, wild Pong Forest. The chapter highlights the community’s intense commitment to strict practice, which allowed the forest monastic lineage to successfully take root despite early hardships and friction with urban monastics.
