10. A Broader Canvas


high-definition creative commons posters depicting A Broader Canvas from Stillness Flowing, The Life and Teachings of Ajahn Chah together with further information.

This chapter details Ajahn Chah’s historic travels to the West in 1977 and 1979, specifically focusing on his visits to England and America. It explores his interactions with Western seekers, his practical responses to their existential questions, and his universal approach to the Dhamma. His guidance successfully laid the foundation for establishing a thriving branch monastery lineage outside of Thailand.
