12. A Life Expired


high-definition creative commons posters depicting A Life Expired from Stillness Flowing, The Life and Teachings of Ajahn Chah together with further information.

This chapter details the final years, medical crises, and eventual death of the revered Thai Buddhist master Ajahn Chah. Returning to his home monastery, Wat Nong Pah Pong, to spend his final days, he defied expectations by surviving for over nine years in stillness and silence. The narrative captures the profound devotion of his disciples and the serene atmosphere surrounding his passing.
