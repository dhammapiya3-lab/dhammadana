4. Delight


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Delight from The Seven facets of Insight together with further information.

Sustained energy awakens a sense of delight (piti), often described as spiritual rapture or zest. It is a profound, non-sensual joy that arises when the mind becomes deeply absorbed in wholesome practice. Delight acts as a natural emotional lubricant, uplifting the spirit, filling the body with pleasant vitality, and making the meditative process deeply rewarding. It proves that the path inward is one of profound happiness.
