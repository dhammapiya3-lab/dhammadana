Source of the Stories

These storybooks describe the seven factors of awakening, which serve as the essential elements for achieving spiritual enlightenment and inner freedom. This journey begins with mindfulness, a grounded awareness that leads to the active investigation of reality and the cultivation of resilient energy. As these qualities mature, they spark spiritual delight and a deep sense of physical and mental tranquillity. This calm state enables unwavering concentration, allowing the practitioner to perceive truth with laser-like clarity. The process culminates in equanimity, a state of perfect balance and liberation that remains undisturbed by life's fluctuations. Together, these facets function not just as a sequence, but as a harmonious system for transforming the mind.
