Seven Facets of Insight


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Seven Facets of Insight together with further information.

The seven factors of awakening are not progressive steps toward enlightenment, but the very components that constitute it. True enlightenment requires a pure, unprejudiced mind free from attachment and bias. This allows for a total revolution in living—seeing reality exactly as it is. While individual factors can dynamically balance a sluggish or over-active mind, it is the harmony of all seven, anchored by mindfulness, that realizes complete inner freedom.
