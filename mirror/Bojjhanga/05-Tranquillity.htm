5. Tranquillity


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Tranquillity from The Seven facets of Insight together with further information.

As the intensity of delight matures, it naturally cools into tranquillity (passaddhi). This facet represents a profound physical and mental calmness. The turbulent waves of excitement, anxiety, and restlessness subside, leaving both the body and mind deeply relaxed yet fully awake. Tranquillity brings a soothing, effortless peace, smoothing out any remaining mental friction and preparing the consciousness for the deepest states of unified focus.
