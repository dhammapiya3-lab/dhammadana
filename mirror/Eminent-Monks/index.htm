Mogao Style

high-definition creative commons posters from The Biographies of Eminent Monks together with further information.

The illustrations are based on some of The Biographies of Eminent Monks, which is a translation by Tianshu Yang of 高僧傳 (Gaoseng Zhuan), compiled by Shi Huijiao in the 6th century. The selection focuses on those who were particularly engaged in bringing the texts to China and translating them, and chronicles the transformative era of Buddhist transmission to China (1st–5th century CE). These monks and pilgrims were the architects of Chinese Buddhism, navigating perilous landscapes—from the Silk Road to maritime routes—to transport sacred Sanskrit manuscripts to the Chinese heartland. By establishing formal ordination lineages, meditation schools, and metaphysical frameworks, these masters integrated Indian philosophy into the Chinese intellectual landscape. Their legacy solidified Buddhism as a permanent, sophisticated component of East Asian civilization, setting the stage for its subsequent flourishing.
