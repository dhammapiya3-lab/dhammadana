Saṁghadeva (Kashmir)


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Story about Saṁghadeva (Kashmir) together with further information.

Saṁghadeva, a master from Kashmir (Jibin), was an expert in the Abhidharma and the Āgamas. After initially working in Chang'an, he traveled south to Mount Lu and later to Jiankang. His primary contribution was the re-translation and refinement of the Madhyama Āgama and various Abhidharma treatises, which helped correct earlier linguistic errors and clarified complex philosophical points for Chinese practitioners
