Guṇabhadra (Central India)


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Story about Guṇabhadra (Central India) together with further information.

Born into a Brahmin family in Central India, Guṇabhadra was a master of the Five Sciences before converting to Mahāyāna Buddhism. He arrived in China by sea and was warmly welcomed by the Liu Song court. He is most famous for translating the Laṁkāvatāra-sūtra, a foundational text for the later Chan school, and the Saṁyukta Āgama. His scholarly precision and lifelong vegetarianism made him a model of both intellectual and moral excellence in Chinese Buddhism.
