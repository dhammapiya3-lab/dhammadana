Guṇavarman (Kashmir)


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Story about Guṇavarman (Kashmir) together with further information.

A prince of Kashmir (Jibin) who abdicated his throne for the monastic life, Guṇavarman became a renowned teacher in Southeast Asia before reaching China. Upon his arrival in Nanjing, he was treated with great imperial favor. He was instrumental in establishing the formal lineage of bhikṣuṇī (nun) ordination in China. Though he died shortly after his arrival, his translation of the Ten-Chant Vinaya and his exemplary asceticism left a lasting impact on Chinese monasticism.
