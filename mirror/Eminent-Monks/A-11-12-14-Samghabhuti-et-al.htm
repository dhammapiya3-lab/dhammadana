Saṁghabhūti (Kashmir), et al


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Story about Saṁghabhūti (Kashmir), et al together with further information.

This details the stories of several monks in Chang'an, primarily Saṁghabhūti, who specialized in the Abhidharma-vibhāṣā. Under the patronage of officials like Zhao Zheng, he and others like Dharmanandi worked to translate critical Sarvāstivāda texts. Their efforts were central to the flourishing of Buddhist scholasticism in northern China, providing the intellectual framework for deeper doctrinal understanding during the Former Qin period.
