08 Kātiyānī of Unshakable Conviction


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Kātiyānī of Unshakable Convictionfrom the Great Chronicles together with further information.

After aspiring under Buddha Padumuttara, Kātiyānī was reborn during Buddha Gotama’s era. While listening to Ven. Soṇa Kuṭikaṇṇa’s teaching, she refused to leave even as robbers looted her home, prioritizing the Dhamma over material wealth. This devotion moved the robber chief and five hundred men to seek ordination into the Saṅgha. Kātiyānī attained Stream-entry, and the Buddha declared her the foremost female lay disciple for having unshakable conviction in the teaching.
