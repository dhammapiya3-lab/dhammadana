high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Narrow Road to the Deep North from Matsuo Bashō’s Walking Trips together with further information.

In The Narrow Road to the Deep North (Oku no Hosomichi), Matsuo Bashō embarks on a poetic pilgrimage across northern Japan with his companion Sora. Wandering through holy mountains, historic ruins, and scenic bays like Matsushima, Bashō contemplates transience, nature, and ancient history. Experiencing physical hardships, warm hospitality, and spiritual reflection along the road, he captures timeless moments in linked verse and haiku before concluding his journey at Ōgaki.
