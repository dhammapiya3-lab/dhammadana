In The Records of a Travel-Worn Satchel (Oi no Kobumi), Matsuo Bashō reflects on his lifelong devotion to poetry as an artistic mind seeking alignment with nature. Embarking on a journey across Japan, he travels lightly, composing verses, visiting holy sites, and reflecting on ancient masters. Embracing the uncertainty and freedom of the road, Bashō finds deep spiritual meaning, beauty, and poetic resonance in the landscapes and people he encounters.
