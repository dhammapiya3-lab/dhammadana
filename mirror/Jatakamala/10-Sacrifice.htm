10. The Story of the Sacrifice (Śīla)


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Story of the Sacrifice (Śīla) together with further information.

A king is advised by priests to perform a massive animal sacrifice to end a drought. The Bodhisattva, as the king, instead proposes a sacrifice of virtuous conduct and charity, teaching his subjects that true protection comes from morality rather than the slaughter of innocents.
