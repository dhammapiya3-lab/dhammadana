12. The Story of the Brāhman (Hrī)


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Story of the Brāhman (Hrī) together with further information.

A teacher tests his students’ integrity by asking them to steal food to alleviate his poverty. All agree except the Bodhisattva, who argues that there is no such thing as a secret crime because one’s own conscience is always watching, upholding the virtue of honesty.
