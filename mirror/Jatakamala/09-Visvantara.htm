9. The Story of Viśvantara (Dāna)


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Story of Viśvantara (Dāna) together with further information.

Prince Viśvantara vows to give away anything requested. After giving away the kingdom’s auspicious elephant, he is exiled. In the forest, he eventually gives away his children and wife to testers. His ultimate detachment and charity lead to a divine restoration of his family and kingdom.
