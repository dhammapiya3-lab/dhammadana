4. The Story of the Head of A Guild (Dāna)


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Story of the Head of A Guild (Dāna) together with further information.

As a wealthy merchant, the Bodhisattva is tested by Māra, who creates a pit of fire to prevent him from giving food to a mendicant. The merchant fearlessly steps into the flames, which turn into a lotus, demonstrating that virtue overcomes all obstacles.
