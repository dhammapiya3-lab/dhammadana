16. The Story of the Quail’s Young (Satya)


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Story of the Quail’s Young (Satya) together with further information.

A forest fire threatens a young quail (the Bodhisattva), who is too small to fly. He addresses the fire with a declaration of truth regarding his innate virtue. The fire miraculously bypasses his nest, demonstrating the protective power of truth and innocence.
