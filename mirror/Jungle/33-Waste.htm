33 Waste and Thrift


high-definition creative commons posters depicting 33 Waste and Thrift from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

Ven. Ānandasiri recounts a night journey during drought, where a Muslim farmer shares water and pineapple juice. The farmer’s community values not wasting food, unlike Sinhala-Buddhists. The monk uses this to discuss why Sinhala people struggle economically: wastefulness at funerals, weddings, and alms-giving, plus excessive desires. He urges thrift, moderation, and learning from Buddhist teachings and other communities’ practices.
