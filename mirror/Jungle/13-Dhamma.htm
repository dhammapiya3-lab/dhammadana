13 A Dhamma Discussion


high-definition creative commons posters depicting 13 A Dhamma Discussion from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

Minister R. Premadasa and his retinue visit Kutumbigala, staying in Maitrī Upāsaka’s cave. Ven. Ānandasiri engages in a three-hour Dhamma discussion on monks’ role in society, covering how monks serve humanity through non-violence and purity rather than politics, the distinction between monastic life and nationalism, and Buddhism as realization of the Four Noble Truths.
