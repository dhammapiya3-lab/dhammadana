high-definition creative commons posters depicting 05 Medicinal Herbs from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

Grandpa Yahapath Hāmi shares jungle medicinal secrets, including the handun mandala herb that repels bears when uprooted silently. He explains gathering methods requiring silence, avoiding nail contact, and turning one’s back. He also details the powerful heenhabara pill, made on eclipse nights with rare herbs and incantations, plus traditional remedies for snakebites, wasp stings, and plant poisons.
