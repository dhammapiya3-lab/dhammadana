Near the Menik Gaṅgā, the group encounters large herds of wild animals and playful wild pigs running about without clear reason. A fierce wild buffalo charges them, but a one-eyed blind buffalo suddenly attacks and chases it away, allowing the travelers to reach Thilinnawa spring safely. Villager Heenbanda notes wild buffaloes are among the fiercest jungle animals, fighting viciously over territory and water holes.
