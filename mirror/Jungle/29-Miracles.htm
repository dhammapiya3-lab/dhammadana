29 The True Miracle


high-definition creative commons posters depicting 29 The True Miracle from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

When visitors ask why meditation does not grant the power to travel through the air, Ven. Ānandasiri dismisses the idea as foolish, noting that crows, airplane passengers, and birds all share that physical ability without spiritual benefit. Instead, true “flying through the air” in the Noble Discipline refers to ethical and spiritual mastery across forty-four methods, such as doing merit, refraining from harm, and cultivating the Noble Eightfold Path.
