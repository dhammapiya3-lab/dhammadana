15 Lost in the Jungle


high-definition creative commons posters depicting 15 Lost in the Jungle from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

While clearing weeds near the ruined central dagaba, a lazy young helper from Warakapola gets lost and wanders in circles for eight days. Surviving on stream water and wild berries while tearing up his clothes to leave a trail, he is eventually discovered by a farmer near Wadagama and nursed back to health by Panama villagers before returning to the hermitage.
