23 A Trip to Kataragama


high-definition creative commons posters depicting 23 A Trip to Kataragama from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

Leading a pilgrimage on foot from Kutumbigala to Kataragama via Yala, the group faces challenges including crossing the rising Kumbukkan Oya and encountering crocodiles, elephants, buffaloes, and leopards. A fearful participant named Deniyaye struggles throughout, mistaking a stick for a crocodile’s tail and burning his umbrella frame in the campfire during rain. After reaching Sella-Kataragama, Deniyaye abruptly departs by bus without saying goodbye.
