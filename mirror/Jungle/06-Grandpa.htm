Centenarian Grandpa Samithambi, a former exorcist and physician known for his robust appetite and immense strength, visits Ven. Ānandasiri after becoming a novice monk. He recounts historical tales, including how a powerful wrestler captured a ferocious Veddah of Karambagala for the Rate Mahattaya, and how Government Agent Kumaraswami’s 1944 jungle loss led to villagers receiving land.
