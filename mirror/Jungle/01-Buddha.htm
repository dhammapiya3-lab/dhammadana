01 The Buddha in the Jungle


high-definition creative commons posters depicting 01 The Buddha in the Jungle from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

Upāsaka Maitrī shares a protective Pāḷi stanza learned in a dream that repels a giant bear during forest meditation. Farmer Naang Hāmi explains local history and villagers’ devotion to deities like Saman and Kataragama. Missionaries failed to convert the community due to ancestral ties and opposition to vices. The Buddha is described as the supreme teacher free from worldly desires.
