Traveling toward Panama, the group approaches a heavily guarded timber camp where their leader is briefly detained so the men can request a visit from the monks. Commander Mr. Clerk warmly welcomes them, provides provisions, and arranges a late-night Dhamma discussion and morning alms. Guided along twig-paths, knife-hacked paths, and elephant-paths, the group faces a charging wild buffalo and discovers ancient ruins at Nalitta before reaching Salawa Eliya near Kutumbigala.
