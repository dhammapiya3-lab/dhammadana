Traveling near the coast, the group encounters an unusual red elephant that had covered itself with red mud from a termite mound. Further along the sand-dunes, Ven. Ānandasiri observes Dalaputtuwa, a rare wild elephant with heavily crossed tusks hindering its feeding, using the moment to teach about the burdens of excessive possessions and compassion for suffering animals. Later, they watch a herd resting against trees after rescuing a fallen companion from mud, though Upāsaka Maitrī inadvertently scares them away.
