14 The Sky-Cave


high-definition creative commons posters depicting 14 The Sky-Cave from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

Exploring archaeological remains around Bambaragastalawa, Ven. Ānandasiri and Tamil helper Sinniah locate the path to the sacred 300-foot Ahaslena sky-cave. Inside, they admire panoramic views, copy ancient Brāhmī inscriptions identifying the site as Nāga Pabbata, and find Veddah drawings. Later, with Forest Reserves Director Chandra Jayawardane, they camp there and watch a bear bathe, roll on a coat, and inspect their provisions before fleeing.
