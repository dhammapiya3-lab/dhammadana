26 Misconceptions about Gods


high-definition creative commons posters depicting 26 Misconceptions about Gods from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

Conversing with Panama elder Mutta about folk tales of gods fleeing Māra, Ven. Ānandasiri explains how villagers anthropomorphize divine figures based on forest rangers. He connects this tendency to widespread human misconceptions about Arahants and supernatural phenomena. Additionally, hermitage inhabitants frequently encounter inexplicable jungle illusions, mysterious caves and buildings that repeatedly appear from a distance before vanishing entirely upon approach.
