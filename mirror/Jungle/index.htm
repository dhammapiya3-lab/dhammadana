Source of the Stories

These storybooks recount Ven. Ānandasiri’s years at Kudumbigala, a remote Sri Lankan forest hermitage. Through encounters with bears, leopards, elephants, buffaloes, and crocodiles, he shows jungle life as a school for Dhamma: fear, visions, and spirits are traced to mental defilements rather than supernatural causes. Elders like Upāsaka Maitrī and Grandpa Yahapath Hāmi embody traditional wisdom and devotion. Historical tales, pilgrimages, lost helpers, fugitives, and visits from ministers and scholars frame deeper teachings on Nibbāna as the extinguishing of desire, true refuge, thrift, and the dangers of waste. Throughout, the theme is consistent: genuine Buddhism is lived, not merely studied, and the forest remains an ideal training ground for liberation.
