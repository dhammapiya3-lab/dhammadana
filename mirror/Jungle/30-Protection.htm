30 Protection at Night


high-definition creative commons posters depicting 30 Protection at Night from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

Meditating on the beach, Ven. Ānandasiri sensed danger, rose with a flashlight, and found an enormous crocodile just four yards from a sleeping devotee. With Yahapath Hāmi’s approach, the crocodile slipped into the sea. Responding to the belief that devas intervened, Ven. Ānandasiri explained that true protection stems from the power of Dharma and wholesome karma, concluding that trusting one’s life to them brings ultimate peace.
