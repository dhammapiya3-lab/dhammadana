Accompanied by retired Chief Justice Mr. Hema Basnayake on jungle tours, Ven. Ānandasiri treated villagers with traditional medicine and navigated difficult terrain by jeep and bullock cart. During discussions on scriptural study and meditation, Ven. Ānandasiri explained that true insight into impermanence and suffering is essential for realizing Nibbāna, emphasizing that ideal monks integrate both study and practice regardless of whether they reside in forests or towns.
