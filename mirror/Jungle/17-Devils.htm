17 The Devils We See


high-definition creative commons posters depicting 17 The Devils We See from Twenty Five Years of Life in the Jungle together with further information.

Many visitors and helpers recount frightening encounters with supposed devils residing in the caves, often resulting in terror or fainting. Ven. Ānandasiri explains such fears stem from conditioned imaginations rather than canonical truths, noting true devils are merely brave men or figures of speech. After discussing these misconceptions and studying the Buddha’s teachings on fearlessness, troubled visitors learn to overcome their anxieties and find deep satisfaction in jungle solitude.
