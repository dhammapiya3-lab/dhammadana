17. Three Stories About Birds


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Three Stories About Birds from the Pañcatantra together with further information.

These tales discuss the importance of unity and caution. One story tells of a flock of pigeons escaping a net by flying together, while another describes a bird that loses its nest because it trusted a predator. The underlying message is that while strength is found in numbers, one must always be vigilant against those who offer help with hidden motives.
