10. Three Stories About Foxes


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Three Stories About Foxes from the Pañcatantra together with further information.

These tales focus on the fox’s legendary cunning. In one, a fox escapes a hunter by feigning death; in another, a fox tricks a crane into a lopsided dinner arrangement. The stories emphasize that in a world of predators and traps, wit is the most valuable survival tool, though it often comes at the expense of honesty or friendship.
