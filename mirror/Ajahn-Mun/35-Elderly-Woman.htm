35 An Elderly Woman


high-definition creative commons posters depicting An Elderly Woman from The Life of Ajahn Mun together with further information.

An elderly white-robed lay woman, nearly eighty and an accomplished meditator with mind-reading powers, often consulted Ajahn Mun despite her painful walks. She saw radiant auras around all the monks and visited the brahma realms. Ajahn Mun explained why only monks appear there and advised her to focus on internal investigation rather than external phenomena. He lived at Ban Nong Pheu five years, partly for her benefit.
