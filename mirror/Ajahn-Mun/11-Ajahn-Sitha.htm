high-definition creative commons posters depicting Ajahn Sitha from The Life of Ajahn Mun together with further information.

Ajahn Mun’s compassion for fellow monks led him to leave Sarika Cave after years of practice, his stomach ailment cured and his path certain. Before departing, he confirmed by mind-reading that Chao Khun Upāli was contemplating dependent origination. He then wandered through Burma, Chiang Mai, and Laos, where his companion Ajahn Sitha was watched by a Bengal tiger that roared when silently wished away, then vanished.
