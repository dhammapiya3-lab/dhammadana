Asked about past-life connections between spouses, Ajahn Mun says people mostly marry blindly, driven by defilements, and that past-life bonds are hard to verify. Couples wishing to reunite must share views, keep faithfulness, and act on their intention—mere wishing accomplishes nothing. A devoted husband’s questions about Nibbāna and heavenly realms prompt Ajahn Mun to laugh and teach that happiness varies by realm and attainment.
