33 The Bodhi Tree


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Bodhi Tree from The Life of Ajahn Mun together with further information.

Ajahn Mun instructed monks to keep strict discipline, develop concentration and wisdom, and behave irreproachably. The chapter details his rigorous daily routine—little sleep, constant meditation—and contrasts his diligence with others. It lists his senior disciples who became like Bodhi trees in their own right, and explains how difficult it is to cultivate a monk in Dhamma, requiring persistent effort against defilements.
