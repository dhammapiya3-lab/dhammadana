15 His Way of Teaching


high-definition creative commons posters depicting His Way of Teaching from The Life of Ajahn Mun together with further information.

Ajahn Mun’s growing following lived scattered in the forest, assembling on observance days for Pāṭimokkha recitation and Dhamma talks lasting hours. Monks revered his instructions as lifeblood, accepting his assigned caves with conviction. He trained fearful monks by isolating them in frightening places, taught laypeople at four p.m. and monks from seven, and gave personal interviews about meditation visions—rare external phenomena requiring expert guidance.
