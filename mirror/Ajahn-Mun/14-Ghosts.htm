14 Ghosts


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Ghosts from The Life of Ajahn Mun together with further information.

A disciple terrified of ghosts camped unknowingly beside a charnel ground. As darkness fell, imagining ghosts surrounding him, he forced himself to approach the burning corpse and meditate on death. Hearing footsteps and chewing, he nearly fled, then discovered only a village dog eating offerings. Shamed, he vowed to remain until fear was conquered, realizing perception had deceived him. He stayed, conquered his fear, and became a courageous monk.
