07 Sarika Cave


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Sarika Cave from The Life of Ajahn Mun together with further information.

Villagers warned that Sarika Cave’s guardian spirit killed trespassing monks. Ajahn Mun entered anyway. Struck by severe stomach illness, he abandoned medicine, resolving to cure himself through Dhamma or die. Meditating through the night, his illness vanished and he encountered a thirty-foot black spirit threatening him with a club. Ajahn Mun’s Dhamma admonition converted the spirit, actually a terrestrial deva chief, who took refuge and offered protection.
