34 Training Young Monks


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Training Young Monks from The Life of Ajahn Mun together with further information.

The author, Ajahn Mahā Boowa, dozing at midday, dreamed Ajahn Mun scolded him for sleeping like a pig. Ajahn Mun interpreted it as Dhamma’s warning against pig-like tendencies and laziness, encouraging him not to fear his teacher but to fear evil. He also taught that repeating Buddho continuously brings back a wandering mind, like food bringing a dog home, ending meditation decline.
