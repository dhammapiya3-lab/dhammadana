27 A Hypercritical Nāga


high-definition creative commons posters depicting A Hypercritical Nāga from The Life of Ajahn Mun together with further information.

A conceited nāga guarding Chiang Dao Cave criticized Ajahn Mun’s every action—footsteps, stumbling, even snoring. Ajahn Mun patiently explained his harmless intentions and the dangers of fault-finding, urging self-reflection. The nāga admitted its heart gravitated toward degradation and promised to try, though with difficulty. Ajahn Mun then left, and later used the story to teach about character training from childhood.
