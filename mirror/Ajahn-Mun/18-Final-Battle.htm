18 Final Battle for Awakening


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Final Battle for Awakening from The Life of Ajahn Mun together with further information.

In Chiang Mai’s wilderness, Ajahn Mun launched his final assault on ignorance, resolving to win or die. Investigating dependent origination through the night, wielding supreme-mindfulness and supreme-wisdom, at 3 a.m. ignorance perished and the universe trembled. He attained Arahantship—the mind making three revolutions: cutting off, absolute freedom, total detachment. Initially disinclined to teach, he reconsidered the Buddha’s example, resolving to help those receptive.
