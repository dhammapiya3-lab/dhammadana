Source of the Stories

These storybooks provide a comprehensive overview of fundamental Buddhist principles through a series of instructional posters and analytical texts. The collection explores the interconnected nature of reality, emphasizing how the illusion of a permanent self leads to internal conflict and suffering. Key concepts such as impermanence, karma, and rebirth are examined to show how intentional actions and psychological attachments drive the cycle of existence. The materials further detail the process of dependent origination, illustrating how ignorance fuels mental formations and sensory cravings. The sources advocate for the cultivation of direct insight and wisdom to dissolve false identities. By moving beyond temporary mental concentration to genuine understanding, a practitioner can achieve liberation and live authentically in the present.
