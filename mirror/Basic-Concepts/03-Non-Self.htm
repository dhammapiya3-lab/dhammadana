03 Non-Self


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Non-Self from Basic Buddhist Concepts, together with further information.

The text explores anatta (non-self), asserting that the self is a psychological construct created by memory and sustained craving for permanence. This delusion fuels choice and conflict (dukkha) within an impermanent world (anicca). By dismantling various logical and theological arguments for an eternal soul, the author emphasizes that direct insight into the voidness of self (anatta saññā) is essential to the achievement of liberation and ending all unnecessary suffering and conflict.
