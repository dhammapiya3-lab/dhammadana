13 Insight


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Insight from Basic Buddhist Concepts, together with further information.

Concentration (samādhi) seeks peace through methods, yet wisdom (paññā) understands things as they truly are (yathā bhūtaṁ pajānāti). While knowledge explains “how,” wisdom investigates “why”. Conflict (dukkha) stems from the “self” (anattā) craving permanence in an impermanent (anicca) world. True insight (vipassanā) reveals the emptiness of this artificial “self,” enabling immediate, spontaneous liberation by discarding the false to live fully in the present moment.
