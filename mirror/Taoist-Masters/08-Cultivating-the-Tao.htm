08 Cultivating the Tao


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Cultivating the Tao from Seven Taoist Masters together with further information.

Wang Ch’ung-yang draws a circle and dot to explain Wu-chi, T’ai-chi and original nature, then teaches Ma Tan-yang and Sun Pu-erh to eradicate liquor, sexual desire, riches and bad temper, with detailed instructions for the hour of tzu. After months of practice they stop seeking guidance. Wang appears separately to each; his talk of yin and yang copulating offends Sun Pu-erh, who flees and summons her husband, interrupting Ma’s lesson.
