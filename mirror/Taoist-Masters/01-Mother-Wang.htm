01 Mother Wang


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Mother Wang from Seven Taoist Masters together with further information.

On a snowy night Mother Wang turns away two beggars, feeding monks instead; the beggars chide her charity as self-serving. They come to Wang T’ieh-hsin, who shelters them. Calling themselves Gold-Is-Heavy and Empty-Mind Ch’ang, they lead him over a bridge and up a mountain, where Empty-Mind Ch’ang picks seven golden lotus flowers, spirits of his seven future disciples, and gives them to him. He falls from a cliff, losing consciousness.
