11 Beggar Woman


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Beggar Woman from Seven Taoist Masters together with further information.

In Loyang Sun Pu-erh begs as a madwoman until Chang San and Li Ssu come to molest her; hail and thorns bloody Chang San, and the city leaves her in peace for twelve years. In Shantung villagers test Wang Ch’ung-yang’s prophecy of rain on the twenty-third; it rains. He directs a man to his stolen water buffalo and tells a boy to ask his mother, where his brother’s letter awaits.
