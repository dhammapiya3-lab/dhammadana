04 Seekers of the Tao


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Seekers of the Tao from Seven Taoist Masters together with further information.

Nearing forty and childless, wealthy Ma Yü and his wife Sun Yüan-chen sit in a garden pavilion in Ning-hai County, where she argues that riches and heirs are impermanent and urges him to seek immortality, explaining internal alchemy. They resolve to find a teacher and recognize the radiant beggar near the Ma mansion as Wang Ch’ung-yang, who has waited years for their gesture. Ma posts a servant and hurries out.
