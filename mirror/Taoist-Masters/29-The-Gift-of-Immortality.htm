29 The Gift of Immortality


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Gift of Immortality from Seven Taoist Masters together with further information.

Ch’iu wrote Journey to the West in solitude at White Cloud Monastery, warning of the mind’s wild horses and mischievous monkey; Pai-yün answered with Legends of the Gods. Ascending to the Palace of Heaven, Ch’iu found the six disciples awaiting the Jade Emperor’s gift. The Lords ranked Sun Pu-erh seventh. Ch’iu refused the gift until the red-bearded Lord of Thunder pledged to guard all seekers; then the immortal peach ripened.
