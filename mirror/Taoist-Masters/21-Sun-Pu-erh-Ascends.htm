21 Sun Pu-erh Ascends


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Sun Pu-erh Ascends from Seven Taoist Masters together with further information.

Sun Pu-erh, twelve years a mad woman in Loyang, transforms two branches into a jesting couple, drawing the mayor’s decree that she be burned. Rising from the flames on clouds, she promises the city prosperity and is enshrined. Returning to Ning-hai, she shames Ma Tan-yang with Lü Tung-pin’s tale and a boiling bath; he abandons his mansion. At Wang Ch’ung-yang’s tomb he teaches Ch’iu Ch’ang-ch’un nonattachment, then snow traps them.
