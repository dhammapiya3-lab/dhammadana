The Life of Mahāsi Sayādaw

Ava Temple Painting Style

high-definition creative commons posters from the Biography of the Most Venerable Mahāsi Sayādaw written by Ashin Sīlanandābhivaṁsa, translation published by SBVMS in Malaysia.

These storybooks chronicle the extraordinary life and global legacy of the Burmese meditation master Mahāsi Sayādaw, from his intellectually gifted childhood to his final missionary journeys. The biographical excerpts detail his intensive monastic training, his mastery of Buddhist scripture, and his pivotal role as the Chief Interrogator during the historic Sixth Buddhist Council. A significant portion of the text highlights his international influence, documenting how he established meditation centers across Asia, Europe, and the United Kingdom to spread the practice of Satipaṭṭhāna Vipassanā. The records also describe the exponential growth of the Sāsana Yeikthā center in Rangoon, which became a spiritual hub for both local and foreign seekers. Finally, the collection concludes by honoring his 1982 passing and the continued preservation of his teachings by dedicated organizations.
