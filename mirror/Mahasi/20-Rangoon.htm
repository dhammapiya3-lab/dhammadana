20 The Rangoon Centre


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Rangoon Centre from the Life of Mahasi Sayadaw together with further information.

There was exponential growth of the Rangoon Sāsana Yeikthā amidst the historic Sixth Buddhist Council. Preoccupied with high-level council duties, Mahāsi Sayādaw delegated daily meditation auditing to a trusted council of senior disciple-teachers. Managed by lay benefactors, the center’s real estate rapidly expanded from 5.5 to over 20 acres, transforming into a sprawling spiritual complex driven by deep public devotion and prayers.
