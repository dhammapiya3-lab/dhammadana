Conclusion


high-definition creative commons posters depicting the Conclusion from the Mahābhārata together with further information.

Following Krishna’s departure and the destruction of the Yadavas, the Pandavas renounce their kingdom and journey toward the Himalayas to reach heaven. One by one, Draupadi and the four younger brothers fall, leaving only Yudhishthira and a faithful dog. Upon reaching the celestial gates, Yudhishthira refuses to enter without the dog, who reveals himself as the god Dharma. After a final test of his compassion in the underworld, the family is reunited in eternal peace.
