I. The Tournament


high-definition creative commons posters depicting the The Tournament from the Mahābhārata together with further information.

After completing their martial training under Drona, the Pandavas and Kauravas display their skills in a grand public arena. Arjuna’s supreme archery is challenged by the mysterious newcomer Karna, who is befriended by Duryodhana. This event cements the lifelong rivalry between the cousins. The tournament highlights the emerging prowess of the Pandavas, the growing insecurity of the Kauravas, and the complicated social dynamics regarding lineage and warrior status.
