7: Summary and Conclusion


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Summary and Conclusion from the Gentle Way together with further information.

On the final day, the focus is on taking the retreat experience back into the world. Godwin emphasizes that the real retreat begins when they leave. He provides advice on maintaining awareness in the face of family and work pressures. The session ends with a message of encouragement: to continue being a light unto oneself and living the Dhamma daily.
