1: Why We Should Meditate


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Why We Should Meditate from the Gentle Way together with further information.

Godwin introduces meditation as a way of living rather than just a sitting practice. He emphasizes understanding the mind and its reactions to daily challenges. Instead of controlling thoughts, we should observe them with a friendly, non-judgmental attitude. This gentle way transforms suffering into learning, helping us find peace within ourselves amidst the chaos of worldly life.
