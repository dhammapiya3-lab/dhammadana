3: The Importance of the Dhamma


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Importance of the Dhamma from the Gentle Way together with further information.

Godwin discusses how material consumerism and the pursuit of toys fail to provide lasting happiness. He suggests that through Dhamma, we become our own toy, finding joy within. He encourages learning from mistakes without self-criticism and viewing life as the ultimate teacher. Being an island unto oneself means developing self-reliance and befriending one’s own mind and loneliness.
