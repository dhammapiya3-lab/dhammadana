2. Āryādeva


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Āryādeva from Buston’s History of Buddhism together with further information.

Miraculously born in a lotus on Simhala island, Aryadeva was adopted by a king before becoming Nāgārjuna's foremost spiritual disciple. Renowned for his vast mastery of orthodox and heterodox philosophies, he famously sacrificed an eye to a tree goddess while traveling to Nalanda. There, he triumphantly vanquished the formidable heretic Matrceta, defending the Buddhist Doctrine and establishing a prolific legacy of Tantric and philosophical treatises.
