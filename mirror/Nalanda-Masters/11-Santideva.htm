11. Śāntideva


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Śāntideva from Buston’s History of Buddhism together with further information.

Prince Śāntivarman fled his impending coronation after the deity Mañjuśrī claimed the throne in a dream. Ordained as Śāntideva at Nālandā, he secretly received deep teachings from Mañjuśrī while other monks mocked his external routine of eating, sleeping, and walking. Labeled ‘Bhu-Su-Ku’ and deemed lazy, he surprised his detractors by masterfully reciting profound treatises, proving his immense sanctity and internal realization.
