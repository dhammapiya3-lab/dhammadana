III. The Great Lineage of Kings


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Great Lineage of Kings in the Chronicle of the Island together with further information.

This chapter traces the Buddha’s royal ancestry, beginning with Mahāsammata, the first king of the world. It enumerates the long succession of dynasties through various eras, highlighting the continuity of the Solar race. The lineage connects the ancient kings of India to the Buddha’s own family, the Sakyas. This genealogical record serves to establish the Buddha’s noble origin and the legitimate royal foundations upon which the Dhamma would eventually spread.
