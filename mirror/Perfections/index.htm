Source of the Stories

This collection of storybooks outlines the ten perfections found in Buddhist teachings, serving as a comprehensive guide to moral and spiritual development. Each section explores a specific virtue—such as generosity, morality, renunciation, and wisdom—by defining its core characteristics and the mental discipline required for its mastery. The texts further examine the practical application of energy, forbearance, truthfulness, and resolution, illustrating how these qualities protect and strengthen the practitioner. Additionally, the final chapters focus on the cultivation of loving-kindness and equanimity, emphasizing a balanced, compassionate attitude toward all living beings. Together, these sources utilize poster art and scriptural stories to provide a clear roadmap for achieving ethical excellence and ultimate enlightenment.
