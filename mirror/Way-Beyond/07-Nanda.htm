07 Nanda’s Questions


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Nanda’s Questions from The Way to the Beyond together with further information.

Nanda asks about the wise ones who are free from doubt and how they cross the flood. The Buddha answers that the wise abandon craving and conceit, and by understanding the nature of things, they cross the flood. He teaches that one should be mindful, not cling to anything, and thus attain liberation.
