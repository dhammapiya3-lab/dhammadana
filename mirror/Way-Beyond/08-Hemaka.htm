08 Hemaka’s Questions


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Hemaka’s Questions from The Way to the Beyond together with further information.

Hemaka asks about the nature of the world and how to be free from suffering. The Buddha explains that the world is deluded by ignorance, and the wise abandon craving and conceit. He teaches that one should be mindful, not cling to anything, and thus attain Nibbāna.
