15 Mogharāja’s Questions


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Mogharāja’s Questions from The Way to the Beyond together with further information.

Mogharāja asks how, looking upon the world, one is not seen by the King of Death. The Buddha answers: look upon the world as empty, always mindful, and abandon the view of self. Thus one goes beyond death. Mogharāja and his students attain awakening.

A biography of Ven. Mogharāja can be found amongst the section on the Foremost Disciples of the Buddha
