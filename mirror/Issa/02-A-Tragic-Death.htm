02 A Tragic Death


high-definition creative commons posters depicting A Tragic Death from Kobayashi Issa’s The Spring of My Life together with further information.

On a spring morning, young Takamaru drowned while gathering herbs. His body was recovered with flowers for his parents, whose grief challenged their religious composure. During the cremation, the narrator composed a tanka reflecting on life’s tragic transience. The text contemplates whether flowers, like humans, possess a life force and seek nirvana. The narrative captures the fragile intersection of human emotion and nature’s cycles through poignant prose and evocative poetry.
