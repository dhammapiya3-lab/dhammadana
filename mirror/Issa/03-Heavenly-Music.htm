high-definition creative commons posters depicting Heavenly Music from Kobayashi Issa’s The Spring of My Life together with further information.

The text weaves together prose and haiku to explore the intersections of nature and spirituality. The narrator investigates reports of mysterious and beautiful heavenly music, eventually finding profound meaning in a bush warbler’s morning song. Through seasonal poems, the text captures transitions from spring rain to summer moons. It documents travels to Shimabara, observations of cherry blossoms, and personal reflections on illness within a suffering, transient world.
