05-07 Visions of Nature


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Visions of Nature from Kobayashi Issa’s The Spring of My Life together with further information.

The text explores nature and artifice through personal anecdotes and poetry. It describes a friend’s witty prank involving paper peonies hidden among real ones, highlighting the contrast between life and imitation. The author also examines the cultural significance of toads, from children’s burial games to their roles in ancient mythology and poetry. Finally, several short verses beautifully capture various fleeting, atmospheric moments found deeply within the quiet, serene, natural landscape.
