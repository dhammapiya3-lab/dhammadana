06 The Water Rakkhasa


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Water Rakkhasa from the Pāḷi Jātaka stories together with further information.

Prince Mahiṁsāsa and his brothers, Candakumāra and Sūriyakumāra, fled to the Himālayas to escape their stepmother’s schemes. At a pool, a Water Rakkhasa captured the younger princes for failing to define the true Deva-nature. Mahiṁsāsa, the Bodhisatta, confronted the monster. He explained that true Deva-nature belongs to those endowed with conscience, concern, and virtue. Consequently, the Bodhisatta shared this sacred wisdom with the monster to secure his trapped brothers’ freedom.
