43 The Monk and the Viper


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The Monk and the Viper from the Pāḷi Jātaka stories together with further information.

In Benares, the Bodhisatta led five hundred ascetics in the kingdom of Kāsi. One monk adopted a poisonous viper named Veḷuka, treating it like a child. Despite the Bodhisatta’s warning that the snake would kill him, the monk refused to part with his pet. Later, after being left hungry for days, the viper bit and killed the monk. The Bodhisatta taught that those ignoring wise advice are destroyed like him.
