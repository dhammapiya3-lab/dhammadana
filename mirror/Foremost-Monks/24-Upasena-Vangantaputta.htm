24. Ven. Upasena Vaṅgantaputta


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Ven. Upasena Vaṅgantaputta from the Great Chronicles together with further information.

Under Buddha Padumuttara, he aspired to win high esteem. He ordained and was reprimanded for admitting a disciple too early. Determined to earn praise, he meditated and became an Arahat. He gained 500 disciples through his forest-dwelling example. He was declared foremost in winning a following. He died serenely after a snake bite, showing no change in faculties.
