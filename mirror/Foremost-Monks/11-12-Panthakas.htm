11-12. Ven. Mahā Panthaka and Ven. Cūḷa Panthaka


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Ven. Mahā Panthaka and Ven. Cūḷa Panthaka from the Great Chronicles together with further information.

Two brothers aspired under Buddha Padumuttara—the elder for formless absorption, the younger for mind-made images and form absorption. The younger one had been a learned monk who jeered a dullard, causing his own dullness in his last lfie. Buddha gave him a cloth to meditate on staining. He became an Arahat with psychic powers.
