Source of the Stories

The posters illustrate the hagiographies of 41 prominent Bhikkhu disciples of Buddha Gotama, tracing their spiritual journeys across vast eons. Each account follows a consistent structural narrative, beginning with an aspiration in the past made during the era of a previous Buddha, such as Buddha Padumuttara. These individuals performed significant acts of merit and received prophecies confirming they would achieve the title of foremost in specific spiritual qualities. The chronicles then transition to their final existence, describing their noble births, their eventual renunciation of worldly life, and their attainment of Arahatship. Notable figures highlighted include Ven. Koṇḍañña, the first to realize the Four Truths, and Ven. Cūḷa Panthaka, who excelled in creating mind-made bodies despite initial struggles with memorization. Through these stories, the sources emphasize the themes of karmic continuity, the power of focused resolve, and the diverse paths to enlightenment within the Buddhist tradition.
