10. Ven. Kaccāyana


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Ven. Kaccāyana from the Great Chronicles together with further information.

In Buddha Padumuttara’s time, he aspired to be foremost in elaborating brief teachings. He donated gold bricks to Buddha Kassapa’s shrine, wishing for golden complexion. In his final life as a Brahmin chaplain, he ordained with seven companions and became an Arahat. He was declared foremost in analytically explaining the Buddha’s concise teachings.
