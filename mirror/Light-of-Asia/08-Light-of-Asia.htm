Book the Eighth


high-definition creative commons posters depicting the Light of Asia, Book the Eighth, together with further information.

In the final book, Siddhartha delivers his great discourse at Kapilavastu. He teaches that neither prayer nor sacrifice brings deliverance, as each person creates their own prison or freedom through Karma. He outlines the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path, leading to Nirvana—the end of suffering and rebirth. His father, Yasodhara, and Rahula all embrace the Law, finding peace in the Light of Asia before Siddhartha continues his forty-five-year mission.
