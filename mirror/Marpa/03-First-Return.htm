03 The First Return from India


high-definition creative commons posters depicting The First Return from India from The Life of Marpa the Translator together with further information.

On his return voyage, the jealous translator Nyö has Marpa’s books thrown into the Ganges. Marpa responds without anger, singing a song of shame, and later teaches the view to Nepalese disciples. At a border town, he dreams of Saraha, who blesses his body, speech, and mind and teaches mahāmudrā. Marpa then sings a profound song at Kyerphu. He visits Nyö to copy texts but is refused. Returning to Lhotrak, he finds his parents dead, then begins gathering gold for a second journey to India.
