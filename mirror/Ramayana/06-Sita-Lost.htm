VI. Sita Lost


high-definition creative commons posters depicting Sita Lost in the Ramayana together with further information.

Ravana enacts a cunning plan to abduct Sita, using the demon Maricha disguised as a golden deer to lure Rama and Lakshmana away. Left alone, Sita is approached by Ravana in the guise of a hermit. He forcibly seizes her and carries her through the sky toward Lanka. The vulture king Jatayu attempts a brave rescue but is mortally wounded. Rama returns to find an empty hut, sparking his desperate, grief-stricken search.
