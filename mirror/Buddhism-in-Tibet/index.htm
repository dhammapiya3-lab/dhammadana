Buddhism in Tibet

Tibetan Painting Style

high-definition creative commons posters from Buddhism in Tibet together with further information.

These stroybooks provide a comprehensive look at the foundational figures and evolutionary history of Tibetan Buddhism through a series of illustrated biographical summaries. The collection highlights the three great Dharma Kings who utilized political power and strategic alliances to establish the faith, alongside Indian mahāsiddhas like Padmasambhava who introduced esoteric tantric practices. It details the emergence of major spiritual lineages, such as the Sakya, Kagyu, Nyingma, and Gelug schools, through the dedicated work of prolific translators and monastic reformers. The texts also honor prominent female masters like Yeshe Tsogyal and Machig Labdron, who shaped the tradition’s meditative and philosophical depth. Collectively, these sources illustrate how Tibet transformed from a fragmented warrior society into a sophisticated center of Buddhist scholarship and practice over several centuries. Through the lives of these saints and scholars, the archives document the systematic preservation and translation of sacred teachings that define the region’s unique religious identity.
